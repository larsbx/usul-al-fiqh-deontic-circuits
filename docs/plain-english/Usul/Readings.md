# Usul.Readings in Plain English

Source: [`formal/lean/Usul/Readings.lean`](../../../formal/lean/Usul/Readings.lean)

This module gathers every shard of formal readings of legal maxims into one import. It is generated from the files in `Usul/Readings/` and has no content of its own.

## 1. What it imports

- **`Usul.Readings.Concordance`**: proofs that two independent readings of Ibn Rajab's qawāʿid 3, 9, 14 and 15 agree, or how they join.
- **`Usul.Readings.Maqasid`**: the maqāṣid al-sharīʿa from al-Shāṭibī, al-Ghazālī, the Rawḍa and al-Kawkab: the ranks, the five, istiṣlāḥ and the order of the maqāṣid in tarjīḥ.
- **`Usul.Readings.Mubashara`**: Ibn Rajab's qāʿida 127 on liability between the direct act and the cause.
- **`Usul.Readings.RajabA1` to `Usul.Readings.RajabA25`**: twenty-five shards of readings of Ibn Rajab's qawāʿid, one module per shard.

## In one line

One import that brings in all readings of the legal maxims: the concordance, the maqāṣid, qāʿida 127, and the twenty-five Ibn Rajab shards.
