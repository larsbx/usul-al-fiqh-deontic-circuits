# UsulTest.FuruqTable in Plain English

Source: [`formal/lean/UsulTest/FuruqTable.lean`](../../../formal/lean/UsulTest/FuruqTable.lean)

Every farq (distinction between two similar cases) that al-Saʿdī grades in his register, F01 to F166, is checked against the meaning of its grade. Six later items that overturn earlier reported ones are checked against the rule that only a ḍaʿīf grade may overturn a bare report. The file is generated by `python -m usul_corpus signature` from `corpus/furuq/*.json`.

## 1. Vocabulary

- **Farq (pl. furūq)**: a distinction the fuqahāʾ (jurists) draw between two masāʾil (cases) that look alike.
- **`Usul.Furuq.Grade`**: al-Saʿdī's grade of a farq.
  - **`sahih`**: ruled apart, and a meaningful feature separates them.
  - **`daif`**: ruled apart, but nothing meaningful separates them, so the ruling is one.
  - **`jamDaif`**: ruled alike, but a naṣṣ (explicit text) or a meaning separates them.
  - **`reported`**: he reports the jurists' farq without grading it.
- **`Grade.apart`**: does his grade say the two cases are ruled apart?
- **`Grade.madhhab`**: do the jurists he answers rule them apart?
- **`Grade.his`**: is the ruling his own? True for every grade except `reported`.

## 2. One pair of checks per item (166 items, 332 checks)

Each item `Fnn` carries its grade, and two guards pin what that grade says.

| Grade | Items | `apart` | `madhhab` | Plain English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sahih` | 96 | true | true | He confirms the jurists' distinction. |
| `daif` | 41 | false | true | The jurists separate them; he rules them alike. |
| `jamDaif` | 6 | true | false | The jurists join them; he separates them. |
| `reported` | 23 | true | true | He records their distinction without his own verdict. |

Representative: F01 is `sahih`, F04 is `daif`, F14 is `jamDaif`, F95 is `reported`.

## 3. Overturns (6 checks)

Six later items overturn an earlier one on a shared case: F134 over F133, F136 over F135, F140 over F139, F142 over F141, F144 over F143, F146 over F145. Each guard checks the shape that `Usul.Furuq.overturn_iff_daif` proves is the only legal one.

| Check | Plain English |
| --- | --- |
| `!Grade.his .reported` | The earlier item is a bare report, not his own view. |
| `Grade.his .daif` | The later item is his own grade. |
| `Grade.apart .reported != Grade.apart .daif` | The two disagree on whether the cases are ruled apart. |

So he never contradicts himself: he overturns only what he merely reported, and only by grading it weak.

## In one line

All 166 of al-Saʿdī's graded furūq and his six overturns are checked to say exactly what their grades mean, with no item where he contradicts his own verdict.
