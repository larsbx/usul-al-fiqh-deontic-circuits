# UsulTest.Obstructions in Plain English

Source: [`formal/lean/UsulTest/Obstructions.lean`](../../../formal/lean/UsulTest/Obstructions.lean)

Every farʿ (derived case) annotated as genuine ikhtilāf (scholarly disagreement) fails the sheaf gluing condition in exactly the recorded way. For each of 598 cases the obstruction checker finds twice as many ordered disagreeing pairs as `corpus/sheaf/obstructions.json` records. The file is generated by `python -m usul_corpus sheaf` from the takhrīj corpus.

## 1. How a guard reads

Each guard has the form `(obstructions [()] (positions rs)).length == k`.

- **`positions rs`**: each reported position becomes a patch over the single farʿ. Equal numbers in `rs` mean positions that rule the same way.
- **`obstructions [()] …`**: lists every ordered pair of positions that rule differently on the farʿ.
- **k**: the expected count. Each disagreement appears twice, as (p, q) and (q, p).

This is the takhrīj (derivation) case of `Usul.Cat.Sheaf`: a case has one settled ruling ⟺ no obstruction exists.

## 3. Shapes of disagreement

| Positions | Expected | Cases | Plain English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `[0, 1]` | 2 | 566 | Two opposed positions: the common case. |
| `[0, 1, 0]`, `[0, 1, 1]`, `[0, 0, 2]` | 4 | 18 | Three positions, two agreeing: only the odd one out disagrees. |
| `[0, 1, 2]` | 6 | 7 | Three positions, all different. |
| `[0, 1, 2, 3]` | 12 | 4 | Four positions, all different. |
| `[0, 1, 0, 3, 4]` | 18 | 2 | Five positions, two agreeing. |
| `[0, 1, 2, 3, 4]` | 20 | 1 | Five positions, all different (Ibn Rajab q160.f019). |

Representative: Ibn Rajab q014.f001 has four positions and 12 pairs. Q016.f005 has three positions, two the same, and 4 pairs.

Across 598 cases of ikhtilāf, the sheaf checker finds exactly the recorded disagreements, so each dispute is a concrete, counted set of conflicting positions.
